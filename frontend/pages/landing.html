Workflow

From Violation to Resolution

From roadside apprehension to digital enforcement record: fast, secure, and fully accountable for both officers and citizens.

01
Search Violator Record

Officer searches by plate number, owner name, or license number. System checks for existing records and detects repeat offenders automatically.

02
Issue Digital Ticket

If first-time, officer encodes details. If repeat offender, system pre-fills from existing profile. Select violation type, enter location, upload photo evidence.

03
Record & Notify

Ticket is saved instantly. Admin is notified. Audit log records the action. Vehicle owner can check their violation online at any time via public portal.

Access Control

Two Staff Roles. Clear Public Access.

Administrators and apprehending officers use authenticated workspaces, while citizens can verify traffic citations through the public portal without an account.

Administrator
Requires login · Full system access

Complete control over the system. Manages users, configures violations, generates reports, and monitors all activity through the audit trail.

  • Manage Apprehending Officer accounts
  • Configure violations & penalty amounts
  • View all tickets, payments, disputes
  • Full audit trail access & export
  • Reports, analytics & dashboards
  • System-wide settings configuration
Apprehending Officer
Requires login · Field access

Issues violation tickets in the field in real time. Requires active WiFi or mobile data connection to search records and submit tickets.

  • Search violators: plate, name, license
  • Issue violation tickets with evidence
  • Auto-detect 2nd & 3rd-time offenders
  • Upload photo evidence from mobile
  • View own issued ticket history
  • Receive system notifications
Citizen / Vehicle Owner
No login required · Public access

Vehicle owners can check their violation records anytime using this public portal. No account or registration needed.

  • Search by plate or ticket number
  • View matched citations, violations, and amounts due
  • Check payment status (paid / unpaid)
  • View citation date, location, and payment status
  • File a dispute against a ticket
  • Submit a concern for administrator review

About Calape

Serving the Municipality
of Calape, Bohol

“ Digitizing the citation process ensures transparency, accountability, and efficiency in every violation handled.

Calape is a first-class municipality in the province of Bohol, comprising 33 barangays with a growing population and increasing vehicular traffic. As the community expands, so does the need for organized and efficient traffic management.

The Calape Municipal Police Station, under the leadership of the local government unit, is committed to maintaining peace and order on our roads. This system represents a significant step forward in modernizing our law enforcement capabilities.

Common questions from Calape residents and motorists about the ticketing and management system.

The system covers common traffic violations enforced under Philippine traffic laws and local Calape ordinances, including reckless driving, driving without a license, expired vehicle registration, illegal parking, disregarding traffic signs, overloading, obstruction, driving under the influence, and helmet law violations for motorcycles.

The system has two authenticated staff roles: Administrator and Apprehending Officer. Citizens and vehicle owners use the public ticket-lookup service without creating a system account.
